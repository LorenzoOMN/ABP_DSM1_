# Scrum Dungeon

Scrum Dungeon is an educational RPG web app for FATEC students learning Scrum and Agile practices through narrative chapters, interactive challenges, and progression gates.

The product turns Scrum concepts into dungeon obstacles and rewards. Its tone is fantasy, instructional, and playful, with the Corvo acting as a recurring guide. The learning rule is that metaphors may dramatize a concept, but the real Scrum or Agile term must always be named clearly.

Primary audience:
- First-semester software development students.
- Instructors and evaluators reviewing the ABP project.

Design register:
- Long-form educational story pages with game UI accents.
- Dark fantasy, pixel/painterly assets, high-contrast parchment and gold/cyan highlights.
- Native EJS, CSS, and JavaScript are preferred over framework-heavy UI.

Important constraints:
- Existing backend routes, APIs, JWT flow, and progression gates must remain stable.
- Chapter pages must remain keyboard-accessible and responsive.
- Educational interactions may be playful, but they must not obscure the real concept being taught.
